Step by Step

The Licensing Process

Five clear steps from your first inquiry to your first patient under the brand.

01
Week 1–2

Application & Discovery Call

Submit your application online. Within 2 business days, PSC leadership will reach out personally to schedule a discovery call. This is a two-way conversation - we evaluate fit, you evaluate us. No pressure, no hard sell. Just a real look at whether this partnership makes sense for both sides.

02
Week 2–4

Agreement Review & Countersigning

Receive your customized term sheet, review with your own legal counsel if desired, and execute the agreement. Territory rights are secured upon execution. We handle the brand identity onboarding immediately after signing - no waiting period.

03
Week 4–8

Onboarding & On-Site Training

Complete your on-site observation training at the Newark, DE flagship. Your custom website is built during this period. Vendor introductions, digital platform setup, and team orientation materials are all delivered. You'll leave with a fully operational playbook - not a binder of theory.

04
Week 8–12

Soft Launch & Market Activation

Your practice goes live under the PSC/AFD brand. Local SEO and Google Business Profile optimization begin. The first monthly coaching check-in is scheduled. You are now the designated provider in your market - with the full infrastructure to start seeing patients and generating referrals from day one.

05
Ongoing

Ongoing Partnership & Growth

Our relationship doesn't end at launch - that's where it gets interesting. Monthly coaching check-ins, quarterly CE sessions, annual in-person summits, access to the private provider network, continued marketing support, and strategic consulting as you scale. We're invested in your long-term success, not just your launch day.

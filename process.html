Step by Step

The Licensing Process

Five clear steps from your first inquiry to your first patient under the brand.

01

Submit Your Application

Complete the inquiry form on the Apply page. Tell us about your background, your current practice, your market, and your vision. We review every application personally — no automated responses, no gatekeeping. Just a real look at your situation.

02

Discovery Call with Dr. Robinson's Team

Qualified applicants are invited to a one-on-one discovery call with Dr. Robinson or a senior team member. This is a genuine two-way conversation — your chance to ask every question you have, share your goals, and explore whether this is the right fit for you and your practice.

03

Review the License Agreement

Approved partners receive the full licensing and/or MSA agreement for review. We walk through every term together — no surprises, no fine print that doesn't serve your success. You'll have full clarity on what you're signing before you sign anything. A downloadable term sheet is also available on the Pricing page.

04

Onboarding & Brand Launch

Once the agreement is signed, your intensive onboarding begins. Your custom website goes live, your team receives training, your marketing systems are activated, your vendor relationships are established, and your clinical protocols are dialed in. Within 60–90 days, your location is fully branded and ready for patients.

05

Ongoing Partnership & Growth

Our relationship doesn't end at launch — that's where it gets interesting. You'll have monthly coaching check-ins, quarterly CE sessions, access to the private provider network, continued marketing support, and strategic consulting as you grow. We're invested in your long-term success, not just your launch.

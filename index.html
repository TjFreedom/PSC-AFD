How the Licensing Process Works

We've designed a clear, guided onboarding process so you can get up and running with confidence — and momentum.

01

Submit Your Application

Complete the inquiry form below. Tell us about your background, your current practice, your market, and your vision for what you want to build. We review every submission personally.

02

Discovery Call with Dr. Robinson

Qualified applicants are invited to a one-on-one discovery call with Dr. Robinson or a senior team member. This is your chance to ask questions, share your goals, and explore the fit in depth.

03

Review the License Agreement

Approved partners receive the full licensing agreement for review. We walk through every term together — no surprises, no fine print that doesn't serve your success.

04

Onboarding & Launch

Once the agreement is signed, your intensive onboarding begins. Within weeks, your location is branded, your team is trained, your systems are live, and you're ready to welcome patients.

05

Ongoing Partnership

Our relationship doesn't end at launch. You'll have continued access to consulting, CE, network collaboration, and strategic support as you grow your brand location month after month.

Founder & Chief Clinical Officer

Dr. Ryan Robinson

Founding Vision

Dr. Robinson built two nationally recognized clinical brands entirely from the ground up - and in doing so, created a blueprint for airway-focused, root-cause care that is now available to providers across the country.

A triple board-certified specialist in Craniofacial Pain and Dental Sleep Medicine, Dr. Robinson authored The Root Cause - a landmark clinical text that has become required reading for providers serious about whole-body airway health.

His vision for the provider licensing network grew from a simple but powerful belief: every patient, regardless of where they live, deserves access to a clinician who understands the root cause of their pain, sleep, and airway struggles.

What Access Looks Like

What You Get When You License

This team doesn't just run the flagship practices - they're the backbone of the licensing program.

📞

Monthly Coaching with Leadership

Direct monthly check-ins with Dr. Robinson's team and the National Director of Operations focused on your growth and efficiency.

These aren't generic business coaching calls. They're tailored sessions focused on your specific practice, market, team challenges, and growth opportunities - with direct input from people who have built and run the same type of practice you're building.

🎓

On-Site Training in Newark, DE

Hands-on observation and training at the PSC flagship practice - see how the system runs in a real, thriving clinical environment.

Seeing is believing. Your on-site training immerses you in the workflows, patient experience, team culture, and clinical protocols that make PSC's model work. You'll leave with a clear picture of exactly what you're building - and the confidence to build it.

🤝

Clinical Case Review & CE

Quarterly virtual CE sessions and ongoing clinical case review with network specialists to keep your skills and systems sharp.

Continuing education in the PSC network isn't generic CE. It's focused, practical training built around the specific clinical scenarios and business challenges you face as a licensed provider - delivered by specialists who have seen it all.

Clinical Independence & Compliance

All PSC agreements are structured to comply with applicable federal and state laws, including corporate practice and fee-splitting regulations. All clinical services remain under the sole authority and responsibility of the licensed providers and practice owners. PSC does not exercise control over clinical decision-making. This is not a franchise model. You retain 100% ownership of your practice.
